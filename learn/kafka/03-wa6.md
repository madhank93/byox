## Core concept

Reading the request rather than answering blind. The header is `api_key`,
`api_version`, `correlation_id`, `client_id` — and the correlation id you send
back is the one you were given. Echoing it is the whole contract.

## Go APIs

- `io.ReadFull` of the 4-byte length, then that many bytes.
- `binary.BigEndian.Uint32(req[8:12])` for the correlation id.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`api_key` and `api_version` are 2 bytes each, so the id starts at offset 4 of
the body.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Read the 4-byte length, then exactly that many bytes, then parse. The header
is fixed-width up to the client id.
</details>
