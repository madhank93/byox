## What this stage teaches

One request, several topics, and the response keeps them in the **order asked**
— each with its own error code, so an unknown topic sits beside a known one
rather than failing the whole request. Partial success is normal in a batched
protocol.

## Go you'll reach for

- A loop over the requested topics, emitting one response block each.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The reply's topic count always equals the request's.
</details>
