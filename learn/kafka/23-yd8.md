## What this stage teaches

Many records arrive in one batch and are written as one batch — the producer
already did the grouping, and the broker preserves it. Kafka's throughput comes
from that: one network round trip and one append for many messages.

## Go you'll reach for

- The same single append of whatever the request carried.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The record count is inside the batch header; you do not need to split them.
</details>
