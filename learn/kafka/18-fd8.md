## What this stage teaches

Several record batches sit back to back in the segment file, and they are
returned together. Batching is the other half of Kafka's throughput story: the
per-message overhead is amortised across a batch, on disk and on the wire
alike.

## Go you'll reach for

- The whole file's bytes, unchanged.
