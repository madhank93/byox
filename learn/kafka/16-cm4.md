## What this stage teaches

A topic that exists but has no records: error code 0, and an empty record set.
"Known but empty" and "unknown" are different answers, and a consumer needs to
tell them apart — one means wait, the other means something is wrong.

## Go you'll reach for

- A null compact records field, written as the varint `0`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The partition still appears in the response, with no error.
</details>
