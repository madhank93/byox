## What this stage teaches

One request can carry data for several partitions, and each is a **separate
log file** with its own offsets and its own error code. Partitions are
independent by design — that independence is what lets Kafka scale a topic
across brokers, and why ordering is only ever guaranteed within one.

## Go you'll reach for

- A loop over the request's partitions, appending to each log.
