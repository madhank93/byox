## What this stage teaches

One connection carries many requests, so handling becomes a loop. Because every
message is length-prefixed, reading the next one needs no resynchronisation —
the framing that looked like bookkeeping at stage 2 is what makes this stage
nearly free.

## Go you'll reach for

- A `for` loop around read-and-respond, ending on `io.EOF`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Each request carries its own correlation id; do not cache the first one.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Loop reading requests until EOF. Length prefixes mean no resynchronisation is
ever needed between them.
</details>
