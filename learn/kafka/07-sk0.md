## Core concept

Many clients at once: a goroutine per connection. Nothing is shared yet, so no
locks are needed — but this is the stage that decides it. Anything you later
keep at package scope becomes shared mutable state across every client.

## Go APIs

- `go handleConn(conn)` in the accept loop.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Each connection needs its own read buffer.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Move the read buffer and any parse state inside the handler. Anything left
at package scope is now shared by every connected client.
</details>
<details><summary>Implementation</summary>

`go handleConn(conn)` in the accept loop, with `defer conn.Close()` at the
top of the handler.
</details>
