## What this stage teaches

Several columns, joined by `|`, in the order the **query** asked for — not the
order they sit in the record. That distinction is projection: the stored layout
and the result shape are independent, which is why `SELECT b, a` is a
reordering rather than an error.

## Go you'll reach for

- A slice of column indexes built from the query, applied per row.
