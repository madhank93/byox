## What this stage teaches

The three encodings that carry the rest of the course.

**Cell pointers**: after the page header comes an array of 2-byte offsets, one
per cell, pointing into the page. Cells grow from the *end* of the page toward
the middle, so the offsets are unordered — follow them, never walk linearly.

**Varints**: big-endian, 7 bits per byte, high bit meaning "continue", up to 9
bytes. Used for every size and row id in the format.

**Records**: a header of serial-type varints, then the values. The serial type
encodes both type and size — and the part nobody guesses: for `N >= 12`, even
means a blob of `(N-12)/2` bytes and odd means text of `(N-13)/2`.

## Go you'll reach for

- A varint reader returning value and bytes consumed.
- A record reader turning serial types into sizes.
