## What this stage teaches

A `WHERE` clause filters rows before they are projected, and the order matters
for a subtle reason: you may filter on a column the query does not select. So
the pipeline is decode the whole record, test the predicate, *then* pick the
requested columns.

## Go you'll reach for

- A parsed `column = 'value'` predicate compared per row.
