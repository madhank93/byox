## What this stage teaches

Many commands, appended in the order they were applied. Order is the invariant
that makes replay correct: `SET k 1` then `SET k 2` replays to `2`, and the
reverse replays to `1`. With a goroutine per connection, "the order they were
applied" is only well-defined if the apply and the append happen under the same
lock.

## Go you'll reach for

- The same open file handle, written to per command.
- The keyspace mutex, held across apply-then-append.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

No separators, no framing beyond RESP itself — commands are concatenated.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

If you append outside the lock, two goroutines can apply in one order and log in
the other. The log then replays to a state the server never had.
</details>
