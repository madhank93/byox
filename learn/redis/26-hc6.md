## What this stage teaches

`--replicaof "<host> <port>"` makes the same binary start as a replica, and the
role becomes a first-class piece of server state. From here the program has two
modes that differ in behaviour, not just in what they report — and keeping that
difference in one flag rather than scattered conditionals is what makes the next
fifteen stages tractable.

## Go you'll reach for

- `flag.String("replicaof", "", "")`, split on the space.
- A `role` field on your server struct: `"master"` or `"slave"`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`role:slave` — the protocol uses the old term, whatever the docs now say.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Store the master's host and port at parse time. Stage 28 dials them.
</details>
