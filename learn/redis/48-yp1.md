## What this stage teaches

`-` means "the smallest possible id" — a sentinel standing in for a bound rather
than naming one. Handling it by substitution (`-` becomes `0-0`) keeps the range
logic single-path, which is the general lesson: normalise sentinels at the edge,
don't branch on them in the middle.

## Go you'll reach for

- A parse step mapping `-` to the minimum id before the range scan.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`XRANGE key - <end>` returns everything up to and including `end`.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

One `parseRangeID(s string, isStart bool)` handling `-`, `+`, a bare
millisecond, and a full `ms-seq` keeps every caller simple.
</details>
