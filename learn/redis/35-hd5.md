title: Multi-replica propagation
concepts: [concurrency]

## Core concept

One replica becomes many, and the interesting part is what you hold while
iterating. Copy the replica slice under the list lock, then write to each
outside it — writing while holding the list lock means one slow or dead replica
blocks every new replica from registering.

## Go APIs

- A copied `[]*replica` under the list lock, then a loop over the copy.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Every replica gets every write, in the same order.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Decide what a failed write means. Dropping the replica from the list is
reasonable; ignoring the error and retrying forever is not.
</details>

<details><summary>Implementation</summary>

Copy the replica slice under its lock, release, then write. Holding the
list lock across the writes blocks a new replica from registering behind the
slowest existing one.
</details>
