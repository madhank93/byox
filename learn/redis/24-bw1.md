## What this stage teaches

A `--port` flag, which sounds trivial and is the precondition for everything
that follows: replication needs two servers on one machine, so the port can no
longer be hardcoded at 6379.

## Go you'll reach for

- `flag.Int("port", 6379, "")`, then `fmt.Sprintf("0.0.0.0:%d", port)`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Default stays 6379 when the flag is absent.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Keep the port in the same config map as `dir` and `appendonly` — `REPLCONF
listening-port` at stage 28 needs to send it.
</details>
