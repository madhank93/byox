## What this stage teaches

`XREAD` looks like `XRANGE` and differs in one decisive way: its id bound is
**exclusive**. `XRANGE` answers "give me this range"; `XREAD` answers "give me
what's new since the last thing I saw" — and a consumer that re-received its own
last entry every call would loop forever.

The reply nests one level deeper too, since it can cover several streams:
`[[streamName, [entries…]], …]`.

## Go you'll reach for

- The range scan, with `>` instead of `>=` on the lower bound.
- The nested-array encoder from `XRANGE`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`XREAD streams <key> <id>` — the literal word `streams` separates the keys from
the ids.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Keep `XRANGE` and `XREAD` sharing one scan function with an `exclusive bool`,
rather than two nearly-identical scans that drift apart.
</details>
