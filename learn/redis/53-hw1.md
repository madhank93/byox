## What this stage teaches

`BLOCK 0` blocks indefinitely. Zero-as-infinity is a convention worth
recognising — it appears again in `BLPOP` at stage 84 — and it means the
deadline check has to be conditional rather than a comparison against a computed
zero deadline.

## Go you'll reach for

- The stage-52 loop, with the deadline check skipped when the timeout is 0.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Compute a deadline only when the timeout is non-zero.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Still watch for the client disconnecting — an infinite wait on a dead connection
leaks a goroutine for the life of the process.
</details>
