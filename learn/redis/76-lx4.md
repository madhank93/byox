## What this stage teaches

`RPUSH key a b c` appends all three, in order, and returns the length once at
the end. Variadic commands exist to cut round trips — three appends in one
command is one network round trip instead of three, which for an in-memory store
is most of the cost.

## Go you'll reach for

- `append(list, args[2:]...)`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

One reply for the whole command, holding the final length.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Everything from `args[2]` onwards is an element. No option parsing here.
</details>
