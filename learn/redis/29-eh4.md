## What this stage teaches

Two `REPLCONF` commands: `listening-port <port>` tells the master where this
replica accepts connections, `capa psync2` advertises what the replica
understands. This is capability negotiation — the mechanism that lets a protocol
gain features without breaking older peers, and the reason `psync2` has a
version number in its name at all.

## Go you'll reach for

- The same encoder, twice, reading `+OK\r\n` after each.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Send them in order, and read the reply to each before sending the next.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

The port sent is *this replica's* listening port, from stage 24 — not the
master's.
</details>
