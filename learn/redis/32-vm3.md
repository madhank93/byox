## What this stage teaches

Answering `PSYNC` with `+FULLRESYNC <replid> 0`. The master is committing to a
contract here: it has told the replica which history it is joining and at which
offset, and everything it sends afterwards continues from that point.

## Go you'll reach for

- `fmt.Sprintf("+FULLRESYNC %s %d\r\n", replID, 0)`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The id is the same `master_replid` your `INFO` reports. They must match.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Mark this connection as a replica when `PSYNC` arrives. It never sends normal
commands again — from here it is a destination.
</details>
