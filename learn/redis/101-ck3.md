## What this stage teaches

Longitude spans the full ±180°, but latitude is clamped to ±85.05112878 — not
±90. That number is where the Web Mercator projection's y-coordinate goes to
infinity, so the poles are simply outside the representable range of the
encoding. It is a limit of the storage scheme showing through the API.

## Go you'll reach for

- Range checks, and an exact error message:
  `-ERR invalid longitude,latitude pair <lon>,<lat>`

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Both bounds are inclusive.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Validate before encoding. An out-of-range latitude produces a garbage score
rather than an obvious failure.
</details>
