## What this stage teaches

`WATCH` inside `MULTI` is an error: `-ERR WATCH inside MULTI is not allowed`.
The reason is structural — watching is what you do *before* deciding what to
queue, so a watch registered after the decision could never protect anything.

## Go you'll reach for

- A check for `inMulti` in the `WATCH` case, before the queueing branch.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`WATCH` joins `MULTI`/`EXEC`/`DISCARD` as a command that is never queued.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Keep one list of control commands the queueing branch skips, so adding
`UNWATCH` at stage 71 is a one-word change.
</details>
