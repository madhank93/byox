## What this stage teaches

`MULTI` opens a transaction, and the state it creates belongs to **one
connection** — not to the server. That is a new category of state in this
program: until now everything was either per-connection and trivial (a reader) or
global and shared (the keyspace). A transaction is per-connection and
consequential.

## Go you'll reach for

- Fields on your per-connection struct: `inMulti bool`, `queued [][]string`.
- `+OK\r\n`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`MULTI` itself only sets the flag. Nothing is queued yet.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Put the flag beside the connection's reader and writer. Anything global here
breaks the moment two clients open transactions at once.
</details>
