## What this stage teaches

One pair becomes many, which turns your decoder from a straight line into a
loop — and a loop needs a correct stopping condition. RDB gives you two: the
`0xFB` resize hint tells you how many pairs to expect, and the `0xFF` opcode
marks the end of the file. Trusting only the byte count, or only the terminator,
both work until they don't.

## Go you'll reach for

- A `for` loop over the section, dispatching on the type byte.
- Appending into the same map the server serves from.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Keep reading pairs until you hit `0xFF` (end of file) or another section opcode.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Structure it as: read one byte, switch on it. Opcodes (`0xFE`, `0xFB`, `0xFC`,
`0xFD`, `0xFF`) get their own cases; anything else is a value type, so the pair
follows. That shape absorbs the expiry stages without restructuring.
</details>
