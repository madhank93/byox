## What this stage teaches

`GEODIST` measures the great-circle distance between two members — the distance
over the surface of a sphere, not a straight line through it and not a flat
Pythagorean approximation. The **haversine formula** is the standard way to
compute it, and Redis uses an Earth radius of 6372797.560856 metres.

The result is a bulk string in metres by default.

## Go you'll reach for

- `math.Sin`, `math.Cos`, `math.Asin`, `math.Sqrt`, `math.Pi`.
- Degrees to radians: `deg * math.Pi / 180`.
- `strconv.FormatFloat` from stage 98 for the reply.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Decode both members' scores to coordinates first, then apply the formula.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Convert to radians once, at the top. Mixed degrees and radians produce answers
that are wrong by a plausible-looking factor.
</details>

<details><summary>The API</summary>

Small discrepancies against Redis's numbers usually come from the decode step's
precision, not the formula.
</details>
