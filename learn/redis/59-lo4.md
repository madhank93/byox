## What this stage teaches

`EXEC` without `MULTI` is an error: `-ERR EXEC without MULTI`. The lesson is
that a command's validity now depends on connection context, not just its
arguments — the same bytes are legal or illegal depending on what came before on
this connection.

## Go you'll reach for

- A check of `inMulti` at the top of the `EXEC` case.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`DISCARD` without `MULTI` behaves the same way, with its own message.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Handle `MULTI`, `EXEC` and `DISCARD` before the queueing check at stage 61 —
they are control commands and must never queue themselves.
</details>
