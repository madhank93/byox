## What this stage teaches

The replica connects *out*. Until now your program only accepted connections;
now it dials the master and speaks the protocol as a client — encoding a command
array and reading a reply. Both halves of RESP, in one process.

The handshake opens with `PING`, which doubles as a liveness check before
anything expensive is negotiated.

## Go you'll reach for

- `net.Dial("tcp", masterHost+":"+masterPort)`.
- Your existing array encoder to send `*1\r\n$4\r\nPING\r\n`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Dial at startup, before or alongside starting your own listener.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Keep this connection for the whole session — the handshake, the RDB transfer and
the command stream all flow over it. A fresh connection per step fails at stage
33.
</details>
