## What this stage teaches

Real Redis is single-threaded with an event loop; the idiomatic Go answer is a
goroutine per connection. The trade is explicit: you get concurrency for two
words, and in exchange every piece of state you add from here on is shared
across goroutines and must be guarded.

## Go you'll reach for

- `go handleConn(conn)` in the accept loop.
- `defer conn.Close()` at the top of the handler.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The accept loop should do nothing but accept and hand off.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Everything per-connection — the `bufio.Reader`, any buffers — belongs inside the
handler. Nothing is shared yet, so no mutex is needed *at this stage*; the next
stage that adds a keyspace changes that.
</details>
