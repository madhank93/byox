## What this stage teaches

Now the other side. Your master must accept `REPLCONF` and answer `+OK` — and
because you wrote the replica half in the last three stages, you know exactly
what is coming. Implementing both ends of a protocol is the fastest way to stop
guessing at it.

## Go you'll reach for

- A `REPLCONF` case in the command switch, replying `+OK\r\n`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Accept any `REPLCONF` subcommand and answer `+OK` — the master is not required
to act on `listening-port` or `capa` yet.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Note which connection sent it. From stage 34 that connection stops being a
normal client and becomes a replica you propagate to.
</details>
