## What this stage teaches

Two more `INFO` fields, and they are the backbone of replication.
`master_replid` is a 40-character hex id identifying this master's history — a
replica that reconnects with a different id must resynchronise from scratch.
`master_repl_offset` counts the bytes of the replication stream produced so far,
starting at 0.

That offset is the single most bug-prone number in the course. It counts
**bytes**, not commands, and both sides must agree on exactly which bytes count.

## Go you'll reach for

- A hardcoded 40-hex-character id is accepted at this stage.
- An `int64` offset on the server, initialised to 0.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Both fields go in the same `INFO replication` bulk string:
`master_replid:<40 hex>` and `master_repl_offset:0`.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Real Redis generates the id randomly at startup; the tester accepts any
40-character hex string. Generating one with `crypto/rand` costs two lines and
is closer to the real thing.
</details>
