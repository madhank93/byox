## Core concept

`GEOSEARCH … BYRADIUS` finds members within a distance of a point — the reason
all the encoding work existed. The straightforward implementation decodes every
member and measures; real Redis narrows to the geohash cells the radius touches
first, then measures only those. Both are correct, and the difference is exactly
what the bit-interleaving bought.

## Go APIs

- The decoder from stage 105 and the haversine from 106.
- A filter over the set's members, collecting those inside the radius.
