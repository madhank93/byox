## Core concept

`i42e` — a type marker, the digits, a terminator. Bencode is deliberately
**canonical**: no leading zeros, no `+`, no `-0`, one and only one way to write
any value. That is not fussiness; it is what lets the info hash be a hash of
the encoding rather than of the meaning.

## Go APIs

- Scan to the `e`, then `strconv.ParseInt`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Negative numbers are legal: `i-42e`.
</details>
