## What this stage teaches

The handshake's eight reserved bytes are how BitTorrent grew without breaking:
setting bit 20 (the `0x10` bit of the fifth byte) says "I speak the extension
protocol". A peer that does not understand it ignores the bit and the handshake
still succeeds.

Reserving space in version 1 is why version 2 did not need a flag day.

## Go you'll reach for

- `reserved[5] |= 0x10` before sending the handshake.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Check the same bit in the peer's reply before trying to use extensions.
</details>
