## What this stage teaches

A magnet link carries the **info hash and nothing else** that matters —
`magnet:?xt=urn:btih:<hex>&dn=<name>&tr=<tracker>`. There is no piece length,
no file length, no piece hashes: everything the previous stages read from the
`.torrent` is missing, and the remaining stages are about fetching it from a
peer instead.

## Go you'll reach for

- `net/url` to parse the query; the hash is hex after `urn:btih:`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Decode the hex back to 20 raw bytes — the tracker and handshake both want raw.
</details>
