## What this stage teaches

The tracker is a plain HTTP endpoint, and two encoding details bite. The
`info_hash` parameter is the **20 raw bytes**, percent-encoded — not the hex
string, which is twice as long and matches nothing. And with `compact=1` the
peer list comes back as 6 bytes each: four for the IPv4 address, two for a
big-endian port.

## Go you'll reach for

- `net/url` for the query; `url.QueryEscape` on the raw bytes.
- A loop over the response's peers string, 6 bytes at a time.
