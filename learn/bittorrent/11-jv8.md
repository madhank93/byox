## What this stage teaches

Every piece in order, concatenated into the output file. Real clients pick
pieces rarest-first and download from many peers at once; doing it sequentially
from one peer is correct and much easier to debug — and the difference is
entirely about swarm health, not correctness.

## Go you'll reach for

- The stage 10 routine in a loop; one `os.File` written in order.
