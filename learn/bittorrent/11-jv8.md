## Core concept

Every piece in order, concatenated into the output file. Real clients pick
pieces rarest-first and download from many peers at once; doing it sequentially
from one peer is correct and much easier to debug — and the difference is
entirely about swarm health, not correctness.

## Go APIs

- The stage 10 routine in a loop; one `os.File` written in order.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The file's total length caps the last piece — trim it rather than padding.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Loop the stage-10 routine over every piece index, writing each verified
piece at `index * pieceLength` in the output file.
</details>
<details><summary>Implementation</summary>

The last piece is `length - (numPieces-1) * pieceLength` bytes, which is
usually shorter than the rest.
</details>
