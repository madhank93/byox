## What this stage teaches

A `.torrent` is just a bencoded dictionary on disk: `announce` names the
tracker, and `info` describes the content — its length, name, piece length and
piece hashes. There is no data in it, only a description of data and how to
verify it.

## Go you'll reach for

- The decoder from stage 4, applied to the file's bytes.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`info.length` is the total size in bytes, `info.piece length` the size of each
piece.
</details>
