## Core concept

`d...e` alternates keys and values, keys are always strings, and they are
**sorted**. That ordering is part of the format rather than a convention — and
it is the reason the info hash works at all, since any two implementations
encoding the same dictionary produce the same bytes.

Keep that in mind now: stage 6 needs you to re-encode a dictionary exactly.

## Go APIs

- `map[string]any`, filled by alternating decode calls.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>
