## Core concept

The peer replies with its own `m` dictionary, and **its** `ut_metadata` number
is the one you must use when writing to it. The two ids are independent and
frequently different — assuming they match is the bug that makes the next stage
fail silently.

## Go APIs

- Decode the payload; store `m.ut_metadata` for outgoing messages.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The reply also carries `metadata_size` — the byte length of the info
dictionary you are about to fetch.
</details>
