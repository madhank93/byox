## Core concept

Bencode's string form is `<length>:<bytes>` — `4:spam`. No quotes, no escapes,
and crucially these are **byte** strings, not text: piece hashes are stored as
bencoded strings full of raw binary. Decode by length and never assume UTF-8.

## Go APIs

- `strings.Cut(s, ":")` for the length, then a slice of exactly that many bytes.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The decoder should report how many bytes it consumed — nested values need it.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Work on `[]byte`, not `string`. The binary stages later will thank you.
</details>
