## What this stage teaches

The reply is a bencoded dictionary **immediately followed by raw bytes** — the
dictionary ends, and the metadata piece starts at the very next byte with no
separator. Finding that boundary is why your decoder had to report how many
bytes it consumed, all the way back at stage 1.

Then verify: SHA-1 the assembled metadata and check it against the info hash
from the magnet link. The link's whole purpose is to let you trust bytes from a
stranger.

## Go you'll reach for

- The decoder's consumed count to slice the payload.
- `crypto/sha1` compared against the magnet's hash.
