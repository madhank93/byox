## Core concept

Your first **builtin**, and the reason builtins exist at all: `exit` has to end
*this* process, and no child process could do that on its parent's behalf. The
same logic explains `cd` later.

## Go APIs

- `os.Exit(code)` after parsing the optional status argument.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`exit 0` — the argument is the status code, defaulting to 0.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Check for builtins before searching PATH. That order is the shell's dispatch
rule and does not change for the rest of the course.
</details>
