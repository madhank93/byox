## What this stage teaches

Arguments complete against the **filesystem**, not `PATH`. Same machinery — find
matches, then one/many/none — pointed at a different source. Recognising that
the completion engine and the candidate source are separate concerns is what
keeps the next several stages small.

## Go you'll reach for

- `os.ReadDir(".")` filtered by prefix.
