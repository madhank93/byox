## Core concept

Arguments complete against the **filesystem**, not `PATH`. Same machinery — find
matches, then one/many/none — pointed at a different source. Recognising that
the completion engine and the candidate source are separate concerns is what
keeps the next several stages small.

## Go APIs

- `os.ReadDir(".")` filtered by prefix.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Reuse the one-match / common-prefix / bell logic; only the candidate list
changes.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Point the same one/many/none logic at a different candidate source. Only the
list changes, not the behaviour around it.
</details>
