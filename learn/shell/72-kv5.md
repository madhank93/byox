## What this stage teaches

`declare NAME=value` stores a value, and `declare -p` reports it as
`declare -- NAME="value"` — the assignment form, so the output could be fed
back in. These live in the **shell's** own table: unlike exported environment
variables, children do not inherit them.

## Go you'll reach for

- `strings.Cut(arg, "=")` and a `map[string]string`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>
