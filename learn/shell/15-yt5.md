## What this stage teaches

Outside quotes, a backslash escapes **any** single following character: `\ `
is a literal space that does not split words, `\\` is one backslash, `\n` is
the letter n. It is the most permissive of the three backslash rules, and the
next two stages narrow it inside each quote type.

## Go you'll reach for

- A branch in the unquoted state that consumes the next rune literally.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`echo world\ \ \ script` prints three spaces between the words.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

In the unquoted state, a backslash consumes the next rune literally —
including a space, which then does not split the word.
</details>
