## What this stage teaches

`dir/pre` completes inside `dir`, not the current directory. The word splits at
the last `/`: everything before is the directory to scan, everything after is
the prefix to match — and the completion inserted must keep the directory part
intact.

## Go you'll reach for

- `strings.LastIndex(word, "/")`, then `os.ReadDir(dirPart)`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The completed text replaces only the last segment.
</details>
