## What this stage teaches

`ESC [ B` walks forward, and the interesting case is the end: stepping past the
newest entry clears the line rather than sticking on it. That empty state is
where the user was before they started browsing, so returning to it is what
makes the history feel like a list you are moving through.

## Go you'll reach for

- The same redraw, with an empty buffer at the end position.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Down-arrow at the bottom clears the line; up-arrow at the top does nothing.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Stepping past the newest entry clears the line — that empty state is where
the user was before browsing.
</details>
