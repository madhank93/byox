## What this stage teaches

`echo` prints its arguments separated by single spaces — note *arguments*, not
the raw line. `echo  a   b` prints `a b`, because the shell split the line into
words and `echo` joined them back with one space each. That round trip is why
quoting matters later.

## Go you'll reach for

- `strings.Fields` for now; `strings.Join(args, " ")` to print.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Splitting on whitespace collapses runs of spaces, which is exactly the
behaviour wanted here.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

`strings.Fields` is temporary — stage 13 replaces it with a real tokenizer that
understands quotes.
</details>
