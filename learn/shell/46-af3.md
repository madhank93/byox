## What this stage teaches

`jobs` lists background commands, which means the shell needs a **table** of
them. With nothing backgrounded yet the output is empty — but the structure
decided here (a slice of records with a number, a process and the original
command text) is what the next eight stages fill in.

## Go you'll reach for

- A `Job` struct and a slice of them.

## Hints
