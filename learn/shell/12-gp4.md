## What this stage teaches

`~` is **not** understood by the kernel or by any program — the shell expands
it before the path is used, by looking up `HOME`. That is why `~` works in
`cd ~` and in arguments to any command, but not inside a quoted string.

## Go you'll reach for

- `os.Getenv("HOME")` and a prefix replacement.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Bare `cd` with no argument also means home.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Expand only a leading `~`; a tilde in the middle of a path is a literal
character.
</details>
