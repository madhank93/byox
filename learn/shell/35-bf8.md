## What this stage teaches

Completion works on the third or fourth argument, not just the second. The rule
is positional and simple — complete the word the cursor is in — and if you
implemented stage 24 by finding the last space rather than counting words, this
already holds.

## Go you'll reach for

- The existing last-space split.
