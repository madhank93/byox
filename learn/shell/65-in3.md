## What this stage teaches

`history -w <file>` writes the whole list, **replacing** the file. Simple, and
lossy in a way the next stage fixes: two shells open at once will each overwrite
the other's history when they exit.

## Go you'll reach for

- `os.WriteFile` with the entries joined by newlines.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

One command per line, trailing newline included.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

`-w` replaces the file's contents and marks everything as persisted.
</details>
