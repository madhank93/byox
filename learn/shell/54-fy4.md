## Core concept

Job numbers are **not** a monotonic counter: they are the smallest unused
numbers, so reaping job 1 frees `[1]` for the next job. Small numbers stay
small, which is the point — job control is meant to be typed by hand.

## Go APIs

- Computing the next number from the current table rather than a counter.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

One more than the highest number currently in use; 1 when the table is empty.
</details>
