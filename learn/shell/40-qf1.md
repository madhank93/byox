## Core concept

A completer that prints nothing means no candidates — and the parsing has to
say so rather than producing one empty-string candidate. Splitting `""` on
newlines gives a one-element slice containing `""`, which would "complete" the
word to nothing and look like a bug in the shell.

## Go APIs

- A trim-then-check before splitting the output.
