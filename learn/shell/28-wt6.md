## What this stage teaches

When several candidates share a prefix longer than what was typed, Tab fills in
that shared part and stops. It is the most useful completion behaviour there
is: progress without a guess. Computing it is a character-by-character
comparison across all candidates, stopping at the first disagreement.

## Go you'll reach for

- A `longestCommonPrefix(matches []string) string` helper.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Only redraw when the prefix is longer than the current word; otherwise fall
back to the bell.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Compare the candidates character by character, stopping at the first
position where they disagree. That prefix is what gets inserted.
</details>
<details><summary>The API</summary>

Only redraw when the common prefix is longer than what the user typed;
otherwise there is nothing to add and the bell is the right response.
</details>
