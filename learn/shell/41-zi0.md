## What this stage teaches

The completer is invoked with three arguments: the command name, the word being
completed, and the word before it. That is the interface bash defines, and
matching it means completers written for bash work here unchanged.

## Go you'll reach for

- `exec.Command(path, command, word, prevWord)`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The previous word is empty when the cursor is on the first argument.
</details>
