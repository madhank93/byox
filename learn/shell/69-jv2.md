## Core concept

Exiting **appends** rather than overwrites, using the same high-water mark as
`history -a`. This is what makes several open terminals share one history file:
each contributes its own new lines instead of stamping the file with its own
view of the world.

## Go APIs

- The append helper from stage 66, called from `exitShell`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Entries loaded at startup are already persisted and must not be written again.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Route both exit paths — the builtin and end-of-input — through one shutdown
function, or Ctrl-D loses the session.
</details>
