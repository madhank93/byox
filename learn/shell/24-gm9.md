## Core concept

Completion depends on **where the cursor is**. The first word is a command, so
it completes against builtins and executables; a later word is an argument and
completes against something else entirely. Splitting the buffer at the last
space is what tells the two apart.

## Go APIs

- `strings.LastIndex(buf, " ")` to find the word being completed.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Complete only the final word, leaving everything before it untouched.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Split the buffer at the last space: before it is untouched context, after it
is the word being completed.
</details>
