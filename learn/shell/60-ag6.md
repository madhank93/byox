## What this stage teaches

`history 3` shows the **last** three entries — a tail, not a head — and keeps
their original numbers. The numbers are positions in the whole history, so
renumbering the tail from 1 would break the one thing the numbers are for.

## Go you'll reach for

- `strconv.Atoi` on the optional argument; a start index of `len(history) - n`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

A count larger than the history shows everything rather than erroring.
</details>
