## Core concept

No match rings the bell — `\a` — and leaves the line untouched. The bell is the
terminal's only channel for "I heard you, there is nothing to do", and using it
rather than silence is what stops the user wondering whether Tab registered.

## Go APIs

- `fmt.Print("\a")` when the match list is empty.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Do not modify the buffer, and do not print a newline.
