## What this stage teaches

`COMP_LINE` and `COMP_POINT` give the completer the **whole line** and the
cursor offset, so it can make decisions arguments alone cannot express. Passing
context through the environment rather than more positional arguments is what
keeps the protocol extensible.

## Go you'll reach for

- `cmd.Env = append(os.Environ(), "COMP_LINE="+line, ...)`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Append to `os.Environ()` — replacing it strips PATH from the child.
</details>
