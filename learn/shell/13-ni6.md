## What this stage teaches

`strings.Fields` dies here. Quoting means the tokenizer becomes a **state
machine** over characters: inside single quotes everything is literal —
spaces, backslashes, dollars, all of it — and the quotes themselves are removed
after splitting, so `'a b'` is one word `a b` and `a'b'c` is one word `abc`.

This scanner is the foundation for redirection, pipelines and completion. Every
later stage assumes it is right.

```
the tokenizer is three states:

                    '  (open)
      +------------------------------> [ inSingle ]
      |                                  |      ^
      |                             '    |      | everything literal,
      |                        (close)   |      | even backslash
      |                                  v      |
   [ plain ] <---------------------------+------+
      |  ^
      |  |  "  (close)
      |  +--------------------------------+
      |                                   |
      +-----------------------------------+
                    "  (open)          [ inDouble ]

  plain     : whitespace splits words; \ escapes ANY next char
  inSingle  : nothing is special -- no escapes at all
  inDouble  : \ escapes only  $  `  "  \  and newline

quotes are removed after splitting, so  a'b'c  is one word: abc
```

## Go you'll reach for

- A rune loop with a `inSingle bool` and a current-word buffer.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Quotes delimit, they do not separate: adjacent quoted and unquoted text joins
into one word.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Build words character by character, appending to the word list on unquoted
whitespace only.
</details>
