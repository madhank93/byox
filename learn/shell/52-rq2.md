## What this stage teaches

Several jobs can finish between two prompts, so reaping is a pass over the whole
table: report and drop each finished one, keep the rest. Building a new slice
of survivors is simpler and safer than deleting from the slice you are ranging
over.

## Go you'll reach for

- A `remaining []*Job` rebuilt each pass.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>
