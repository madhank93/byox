## What this stage teaches

`complete -p cmd` reports a command's completion spec, and asking about one
that was never registered is an error message rather than silence:
`complete: cmd: no completion specification`.

## Go you'll reach for

- A map lookup with a miss branch.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The message is exact, colon and all.
</details>
