## Core concept

A background job still writes to the terminal, because it inherited the shell's
stdout. That is why background output interleaves with your typing — the shell
is not forwarding it, the two processes are simply holding the same file
descriptor.

## Go APIs

- `cmd.Stdout = os.Stdout` and `cmd.Stderr = os.Stderr` on the backgrounded
  command.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Leaving them nil sends the child's output to `/dev/null`.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Assign the child's stdout and stderr explicitly; leaving them nil sends the
output to /dev/null rather than the terminal.
</details>
