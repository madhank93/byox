## Core concept

A trailing `&` splits `Run` into its two halves: **`Start`** the process and do
*not* `Wait` for it. The shell prints `[n] pid` and returns to the prompt
immediately, which is the entire difference between foreground and background.

The consequence arrives later: nobody has called `Wait`, so when the child
finishes it becomes a zombie until someone reaps it.

## Go APIs

- `cmd.Start()`, `cmd.Process.Pid`, and a job record appended to the table.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Strip the `&` during tokenizing — it is structure, not an argument.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Split `Run` into `Start` and no `Wait`: print `[n] pid` and return to the
prompt immediately.
</details>
