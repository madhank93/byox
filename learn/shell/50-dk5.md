## Core concept

With several jobs the markers become meaningful: `+` on the last, `-` on the
second to last, blank on the rest. Listing is in job-number order, which is the
order they were started.

## Go APIs

- The same loop over the whole table.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Exactly one `+` and at most one `-`.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

List in job-number order, with exactly one `+` and at most one `-`.
</details>
