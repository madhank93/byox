## What this stage teaches

`>` and `1>` are **not arguments** — they are structure. The shell strips them
during parsing, opens the target file, and hands the child that file as its
stdout. The command never learns it was redirected, which is the whole point of
the file-descriptor abstraction.

## Go you'll reach for

- A scan for `>` / `1>` in the token list, removing it and its filename.
- `os.OpenFile(path, os.O_CREATE|os.O_WRONLY|os.O_TRUNC, 0644)`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Truncate on open: `>` replaces the file's contents, even for a failed command.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Redirection belongs to one command, so extract it per command — that is what
makes pipelines work later.
</details>
