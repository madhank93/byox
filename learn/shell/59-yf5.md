## What this stage teaches

History entries are numbered from **1**, right-aligned in a five-wide field and
followed by two spaces. Humans index from one, and the numbering is what makes
`!42`-style recall possible in a fuller shell.

## Go you'll reach for

- `fmt.Printf("%5d  %s\n", i+1, entry)`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The `history` command itself is in the history by the time it runs.
</details>
