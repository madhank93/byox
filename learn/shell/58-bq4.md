## Core concept

History is a list of the lines entered this session, and `history` is the
builtin that shows it. Deciding what goes in matters: blank lines do not, and
the entries are stored in order because the numbering and the arrow keys both
depend on it.

## Go APIs

- A `[]string` appended to as lines are read.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Record the line as typed, before any expansion.
</details>
