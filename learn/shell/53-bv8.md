## What this stage teaches

Bash reports finished jobs **just before printing a prompt**, not the instant
they exit. That is deliberate: interrupting a half-typed line with a status
message would be worse than waiting a moment. So the reap runs at the top of
the REPL loop, and the notice appears when it can be read.

## Go you'll reach for

- A reap call before printing the prompt.
