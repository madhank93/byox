## What this stage teaches

Headers are `Name: value` lines until a blank line. Two properties bite people:
names are **case-insensitive** (`User-Agent`, `user-agent` and `USER-AGENT` are
the same header), and there may be optional whitespace after the colon that is
not part of the value.

## Go you'll reach for

- A loop of `ReadString('\n')` until the line is `\r\n`.
- `strings.Cut(line, ":")` — cleaner than `SplitN` for one separator.
- `strings.ToLower` on the name, `strings.TrimSpace` on the value.
- `map[string]string` to hold them.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Read lines until you hit the empty one. That empty line is your terminator, not
an error.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Normalise on insert: lowercase every name as you store it, and look up with a
lowercased key. Doing it at both ends is what keeps later stages
(`Accept-Encoding`, `Content-Length`, `Connection`) from each needing their own
special case.
</details>

<details><summary>The API</summary>

`strings.Cut` returns `(before, after string, found bool)` — the `found` flag
tells you a malformed line from a real one.
</details>
