## Core concept

Strings print **without** their quotes, because the tree holds the value, not
the source text. The quotes were syntax; they did their job in the scanner and
do not survive into the tree.

## Go APIs

- The same `LiteralExpr`, holding a string.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`"hello"` parses to `hello`.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Print the literal, not the lexeme. The quotes were syntax and did their job
in the scanner.
</details>
