## What this stage teaches

Each call gets its **own** environment, created fresh when the call starts and
discarded when it returns. That is what makes locals local, what lets recursion
work — every recursive call has its own copy of the parameters — and what
prevents one call from seeing another's variables.

## Go you'll reach for

- `NewChildEnvironment(...)` per call rather than reusing one.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Recursion is the sharpest test: `fib` needs each frame to hold its own `n`.
</details>
