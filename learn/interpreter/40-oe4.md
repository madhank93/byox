## What this stage teaches

A program is a **list** of statements, parsed until EOF and executed in order.
That loop is the top of the whole interpreter: everything else hangs off it.

## Go you'll reach for

- `parse()` returning `[]Stmt`, looped until the EOF token.
- An execution loop over that slice.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Statements run strictly in source order, each fully before the next begins.
</details>
