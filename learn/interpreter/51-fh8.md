## Core concept

Lox has no `elif` keyword and needs none: `else if` is just an `if` statement
in the else-branch. Because the else-branch parses a full statement, chains
come free — a feature that exists only as a consequence of the grammar's shape.

## Go APIs

- The else-branch calling `statement()` rather than requiring a block.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

If chains already work, this stage needs no new code — the recursion did it.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Let the else-branch parse a full statement rather than requiring a block —
chaining then falls out of the recursion.
</details>
