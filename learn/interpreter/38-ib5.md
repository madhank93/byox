## What this stage teaches

Relational operators join the numbers-only club — and `==` deliberately does
not. That asymmetry is the lesson: ordering two values of different types is
meaningless, but asking whether they are equal always has an answer.

## Go you'll reach for

- The same `checkNumberOperands` helper on the four comparisons.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>
