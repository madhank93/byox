## Core concept

Method lookup walks **up the superclass chain**: not found here, ask the parent,
and so on until someone has it or nobody does. That recursive walk is the whole
of single inheritance — the instance's fields are never involved.

## Go APIs

- `FindMethod` recursing into `c.Superclass` on a miss.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

A subclass with no methods of its own still answers with the parent's.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Recurse into the superclass on a miss. That one line is single inheritance.
</details>
