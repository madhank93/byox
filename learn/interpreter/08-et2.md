## Core concept

`<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=` — the last of the two-character operators. Four tokens,
two `switch` cases, no new ideas: the scanner's shape is now fixed, and what
follows are tokens that span more than a fixed number of characters.

## Go APIs

- The same `match` helper.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`LESS`, `LESS_EQUAL`, `GREATER`, `GREATER_EQUAL`.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Two cases, four tokens. After this the scanner's shape is settled; what
follows are tokens that span an unknown number of characters.
</details>
