## Core concept

`-x` and `!x` are prefix operators, and they are **right-associative**: `!!x`
is `!(!x)`. In recursive descent that falls out of the shape of the code — the
unary rule calls *itself* for its operand, and recursion nests to the right by
construction.

## Go APIs

- A `UnaryExpr` with an operator and an operand.
- `unary()` calling `unary()` when it sees `-` or `!`, else falling through to
  `primary()`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`(- 1.0)` and `(! true)` — operator first, space, then the operand.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Self-recursion for right associativity, a loop for left associativity. The next
stage uses the loop.
</details>
