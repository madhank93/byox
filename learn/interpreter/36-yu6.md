## What this stage teaches

The same check for `*` and `/`, with a plural message — `Operands must be
numbers.` Two operands, one message, and the check happens after both are
evaluated, because Lox evaluates both before deciding the operation is invalid.

## Go you'll reach for

- A `checkNumberOperands(op, left, right)` helper.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Note the plural: `Operands`, not `Operand`.
</details>
