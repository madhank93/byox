## What this stage teaches

`-` is numbers-only like the rest, but `+` needs its own message because it has
two valid shapes: `Operands must be two numbers or two strings.` An overloaded
operator needs an error that names both possibilities, or the message sends the
reader looking for the wrong bug.

## Go you'll reach for

- A distinct message in the `+` case.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`"foo" + 1` and `true + true` both produce that message.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Give `+` its own message. An overloaded operator needs an error naming both
valid shapes, or it sends the reader hunting the wrong bug.
</details>
