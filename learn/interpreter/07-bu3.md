## What this stage teaches

`!` and `!=`, the same one-character lookahead as the previous stage. If you
wrote `match` as a helper, this is two lines; if you inlined the lookahead,
this is where that decision starts costing.

## Go you'll reach for

- The `match` helper from stage 6.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`BANG` and `BANG_EQUAL`.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Reuse the `match` helper rather than inlining the lookahead again — the next
stage adds four more operators of the same shape.
</details>
