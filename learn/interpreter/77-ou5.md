## What this stage teaches

`init` is an ordinary method with one special job: it runs on construction, and
the **class's arity is its arity**. A class with a two-parameter `init` is a
two-argument callable — so the constructor's signature comes from a method
rather than being declared on the class.

## Go you'll reach for

- `LoxClass.Arity()` delegating to `init` when present, else 0.
- `Call` binding `init` to the new instance and invoking it.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

A class without `init` takes no arguments.
</details>
