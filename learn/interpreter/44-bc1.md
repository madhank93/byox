## Core concept

`var a = 1;` versus `var a;` — the initializer is optional, and its absence
means `nil`. The distinction is small but it is the reason `VarStmt` holds a
nullable expression rather than a required one.

## Go APIs

- A nil check on the initializer before evaluating it.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`var a; print a;` prints `nil`.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

The initializer is evaluated in the scope that exists *before* the variable is
defined — which is what makes `var a = a;` an error, at stage 68.
</details>
