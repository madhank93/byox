## What this stage teaches

Evaluating `*` and `/` means evaluating both operands first, then combining
them. Order matters and is fixed: **left operand first**, then right — visible
whenever an operand has a side effect, which functions will have soon enough.

## Go you'll reach for

- A `BinaryExpr` case: evaluate left, evaluate right, switch on the operator.
- Type assertions to `float64`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Division is floating point throughout: `3 / 2` is `1.5`, never `1`.
</details>
