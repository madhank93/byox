## What this stage teaches

The shape of a token: a type, the lexeme it came from, and a literal value.
Punctuation has no literal, so it prints `null` — and that three-field format
is what every later scanning stage fills in.

## Go you'll reach for

- A loop over the source's bytes with a `switch`.
- A `Token` struct: type, lexeme, literal.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`(` is `LEFT_PAREN ( null`, `)` is `RIGHT_PAREN ) null`.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Collect tokens into a slice and print at the end, rather than printing as you
scan. The parser will want the slice.
</details>
