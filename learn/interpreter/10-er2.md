## Core concept

Spaces, tabs and carriage returns separate tokens but produce none of their
own. Like comments, they are consumed and skipped — Lox is not whitespace
sensitive, so the scanner simply moves on.

## Go APIs

- A `case ' ', '\t', '\r':` that does nothing.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Newlines are whitespace too, but the next stage gives them a job: counting
lines for error messages.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Skipping produces no token at all, so the loop must tolerate an iteration
that appends nothing.
</details>
