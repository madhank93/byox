## Core concept

`<` `<=` `>` `>=` work on numbers only. Lox deliberately does not order strings
— no lexicographic comparison — which keeps the rule simple: relational
operators take two numbers or they are an error.

## Go APIs

- Four operator cases returning `bool`.
