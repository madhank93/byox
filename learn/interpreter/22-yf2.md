## Core concept

`+` and `-` sit one layer looser than `*` and `/`. The new function calls
`factor()` for its operands, and because `factor()` binds tighter, precedence
is enforced by call order alone — no precedence table, no comparisons.

## Go APIs

- `term()`: a `factor()`, then a loop over `+` and `-`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`52 + 80 - 94` prints `(- (+ 52.0 80.0) 94.0)`.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

The entry point moves outward: `expression()` should now call `term()`, the
loosest level so far.
</details>
