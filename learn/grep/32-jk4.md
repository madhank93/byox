## Core concept

`--color=never` suppresses the escapes even on a terminal. Three settings —
always, never, auto — and this is the middle one: an explicit override exists
because guessing is sometimes wrong.

## Go APIs

- A tri-state colour setting parsed from the flag.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Output is byte-for-byte the plain version.
