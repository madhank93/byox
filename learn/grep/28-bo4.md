## Core concept

`-o` across a whole file: every match on every line, each on its own output
line. Nothing new in the engine — this is the per-line loop and the per-line
match loop composed, which is what you want flags to do.

## Go APIs

- The stage 27 loop inside the stage 17 loop.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

A line with three matches produces three output lines.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

The per-line loop wrapping the per-line match loop — flags composing rather
than each needing its own path.
</details>
