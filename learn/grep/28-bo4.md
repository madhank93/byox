## What this stage teaches

`-o` across a whole file: every match on every line, each on its own output
line. Nothing new in the engine — this is the per-line loop and the per-line
match loop composed, which is what you want flags to do.

## Go you'll reach for

- The stage 27 loop inside the stage 17 loop.
