## What this stage teaches

`a{2,}` — a floor with no ceiling. The greedy loop consumes as much as it can
and retreats no further than the floor.

## Go you'll reach for

- A max of "unbounded", clamped by the input length.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The effective maximum is however many actually matched.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Consume greedily, then retreat no further than the floor. The ceiling is
whatever the input allowed.
</details>
<details><summary>The API</summary>

Represent "unbounded" as a sentinel such as -1 rather than a huge number,
so the comparison stays obvious.
</details>
