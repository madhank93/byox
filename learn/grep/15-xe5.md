## What this stage teaches

`((a)b)\2` — groups inside groups. If numbering follows opening parentheses,
the outer group is 1 and the inner is 2 regardless of which closes first, and
nesting needs no new code. The stage exists to check that choice; getting it
right earlier is what makes it free now.

## Go you'll reach for

- Nothing new, given open-paren numbering.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Count `(` as you parse; the number is the count at that moment.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Number groups as you parse, incrementing on each `(`. Nesting then needs no
special handling — the outer group simply gets the smaller number.
</details>
<details><summary>The API</summary>

Store captures in a slice indexed from 1, sized by the total group count.
</details>
