## What this stage teaches

The skeleton the whole engine grows from. Matching asks two questions: *does
the pattern match starting exactly here?* and *does it match anywhere?* The
second is the first, tried at every position.

grep's result is an **exit code**: 0 when something matched, 1 when nothing
did. Output comes later; the status is the interface.

## Go you'll reach for

- `os.Args` for `-E <pattern>`, `io.ReadAll(os.Stdin)` for input.
- `os.Exit(0)` / `os.Exit(1)`.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Two functions: `match` slides the start, `matchHere` tries one position.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Set that pair up now even though a substring search would pass. Every stage
after this one adds a case to `matchHere`.
</details>
