## What this stage teaches

`(cat) \1` matches the **exact text** the group captured, and that is a bigger
deal than it looks: backreferences make the language non-regular. No finite
automaton can do this, which is precisely why this engine backtracks and why
Go's own `regexp` — a guaranteed-linear automaton — refuses to support them.

Captures must also be **undone when the engine backtracks past them**. A capture
that survives a failed attempt produces matches that look almost right.

## Go you'll reach for

- A capture slot recorded on group success, restored on failure.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`\1` compares text, not the pattern — a group matching `cat` makes `\1` mean
literally `cat`.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Save and restore the capture around each alternative, not just at the end.
</details>
