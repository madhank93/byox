## What this stage teaches

`-o` prints the matched **text** rather than the line, which means the engine
has to report *where* it matched, not merely whether. Until now a boolean was
enough; from here the matcher returns a span.

## Go you'll reach for

- A `findMatch` returning start and end offsets.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

The leftmost match wins, as in every POSIX-style engine.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Keep the boolean matcher as a thin wrapper over the span one — the plain
search path does not need offsets.
</details>
