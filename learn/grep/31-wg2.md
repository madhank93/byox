## Core concept

Highlighting across a whole file — the same composition as stage 28, with the
colour path instead of the `-o` path. Both are the per-line loop wrapping a
per-line behaviour.

## Go APIs

- The stage 30 rebuild, per line.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Non-matching lines are not printed at all, coloured or otherwise.
</details>
