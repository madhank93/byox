## What this stage teaches

Several groups means numbering them, and the rule is **by opening parenthesis,
left to right**. One capture slot becomes an indexed set — and choosing
open-paren order rather than close-paren order is what makes the next stage
work for free.

## Go you'll reach for

- A `[]string` of captures indexed from 1; a group index assigned at parse time.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Assign the number when the `(` is parsed, not when the group finishes.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Number groups as `(` is parsed, and store captures in a slice indexed from 1.
</details>
