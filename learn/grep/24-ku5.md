## Core concept

Until now the answer was an exit code; now grep prints the lines it matched.
Both remain: the status still reports whether anything matched, and scripts
rely on that even when they discard the output.

## Go APIs

- A print per matching line, alongside the existing `matchedAny` flag.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

Print the line as it was, without the trailing newline duplicated.
</details>
