## What this stage teaches

`$` asserts that the input is exhausted. Its mirror-image nature is worth
seeing: `^` constrains where a match may start, `$` constrains where it may
end, and together they force a whole-string match.

## Go you'll reach for

- A trailing-`$` flag requiring the end position to equal the input length.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

`^abc$` matches only the exact string.
</details>
