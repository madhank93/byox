## Core concept

`\d apple` is a sequence: match the first element here, then the rest of the
pattern at the next position. That recursion is the engine's spine, and it is
why elements compose without the matcher knowing what any of them are.

## Go APIs

- `matchHere` consuming one element and recursing on the remainder.

## Hints

<details><summary>Nudge</summary>

A sequence fails as soon as any element fails — no backtracking needed yet.
</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>

Match one element, then recurse on the remainder at the new position. That
recursion is the engine's spine.
</details>
