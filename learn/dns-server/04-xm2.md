## Core concept

An answer record is the question's fields plus a TTL, a length, and that many
bytes of data. For an A record the data is **four raw bytes** of IPv4 — not the
text `8.8.8.8`. Writing the dotted form is the mistake to avoid, and it will
look almost right in a hex dump.

## Go APIs

- The name encoder from stage 3, reused.
- A 32-bit TTL, a 16-bit RDLENGTH of 4, then four bytes.

## Hints

</details>

<details><summary>Approach</summary>
